Help & support

Quick guidance for sign-in, live data, and profile issues.

No live device data

Use Refresh readings, then confirm that a valid reading exists at devices/VB-01 in Firebase Realtime Database.

Firebase access error

Ask the administrator to verify the database rules and confirm that your operator account is approved.

Account or profile issue

Pending account requests need administrator approval. If profile saving reports that the service is unavailable, ask the administrator to restart the dashboard server.

There is no in-app support ticket or contact channel configured; contact your VitaBand administrator for assistance.
